    This browser can't run the EQ (it needs AudioWorklet). Try a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

    Double-click the display to add a band, drag a dot to move it (Shift for fine moves), scroll on it to change Q (the piano under the display shows its note), double-click it to switch it off, Alt-click or Delete to remove it. Hold the mouse still on the background to grab the spectrum: its peaks light up, pull one up or down to put a bell on it (sideways it moves only a little, for fine tuning). Drag across the background to select several dots (Shift-click adds or removes one) and move them together; right-click for copy, paste, duplicate and reset (Ctrl+C/V/D, Ctrl+A selects all). Click a value to type it. Space plays the source. The mode at the bottom left: Zero Latency, Natural Phase (the analog curves, a little latency) or Linear Phase (no phase change, more latency); cuts can be a brickwall there. A dynamic band can listen to the sidechain (SC, pick a sidechain in the player) and work frequency by frequency (SPEC).
